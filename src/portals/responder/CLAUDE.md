# Responder Portal (src/portals/responder)

The repo-wide agent setup — issue trackers, triage labels, domain docs — is in
the root `CLAUDE.md`. Its glossary is `CONTEXT.md` and its decisions are in
`docs/adr/`; its founding contract is `docs/self-made/LGUResponder-persona-PRD.md`,
superseded where an ADR says so.

Its specs, plans and decisions from before the merge are in
`docs/superpowers/`, and its UI design reference in `design/`, both in this
folder. Paths in them are the old repo's.
